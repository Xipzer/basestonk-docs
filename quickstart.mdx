## Trade

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Open [basestonk.io](https://basestonk.io). Any EVM wallet; mobile through WalletConnect. Pick **Base** or **Robinhood Chain** in the header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a token">
    Sort by market cap, volume, holders or age. Each card shows the pair and the tax.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy with what you hold">
    Pay in ETH, WETH or the chain's stable (USDC on Base, USDG on Robinhood Chain). The panel routes into the pair for you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
A few Coinbase B20 stocks on Base have no on-chain market yet. For those you must already hold the stock - the panel says so. [Stock pairs &rarr;](/stock-pairs)
</Note>

## Launch

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    [basestonk.io/create](https://basestonk.io/create). Launching is free; you pay gas and any dev buy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name, pair, opening market cap">
    | Pair | Cap range |
    |---|---|
    | Standard, Base | \$500 - \$10,000 |
    | Gated ecosystem, Base | \$250 - \$15,000 |
    | Any, Robinhood Chain | \$250 - \$15,000 |

    The pair's live on-chain price converts your figure to a pool price when you sign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optionally buy in the same transaction">
    Nobody gets in ahead of you. To your wallet: capped at 10% of supply. Into a [vesting vault](/vesting): uncapped, schedule public on-chain.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Everything else on the form - fees, dividends, buybacks, caps - is on [Launching](/launching).
