BaseStonk pays you for the traders you bring - not once, at signup, but
**on every trade they ever make**, and on the trades of the people they
refer in turn, down five levels.

<Note>
Referrals are a **Base** programme: a link is created by holding USDC on
Base, and rewards are paid there from Base's platform fees. Trades on
Robinhood Chain do not accrue referral rewards.
</Note>

## How it works

- **One link.** Grab it from your
  [portfolio](https://basestonk.io/portfolio). Anyone who arrives through
  it is yours - and so, five levels down, is everyone their network
  brings.
- **Paid per trade, from the real fee** - the platform's on-chain
  reported cut of every swap, not an estimate.
- **It never expires.** A trader you brought two years ago still pays you
  on today's volume.
- **Worth something on both sides** - applying a code takes a slice off
  the platform's cut for the person who uses it.
- **Paid without asking.** Nothing to claim, no withdraw button: earnings
  settle automatically as a **single USDC transfer** to your wallet on a
  rolling schedule. Balances under \$0.50 roll forward until they are
  worth sending. Your running accrual is always in your portfolio.

## The size of what you are sharing in

The platform's cut is **0.5% to 1% of every dollar traded** (see
[Fees](/fees)) - and your earnings are a share of that cut, across your
whole network's volume. The pool your network generates:

| Your network's daily volume | Platform fees it generates per year |
|---|---|
| \$100k | \$182k - \$365k |
| \$500k | \$912k - \$1.8M |
| \$1M | \$1.8M - \$3.65M |
| \$5M | \$9.1M - \$18.2M |

Your share of that pool is attached to your code and shown in your
portfolio - one number, for your wallet, on your earnings.

## Five levels, honestly counted

Most multi-level schemes decay to nothing by the third hop, because each
level takes a fraction *of the fraction below it*. This one does not:
**every level's share is a share of the same trade fee.** Depth five is a
smaller share than depth one - but of the whole fee, so a deep network
still pays.

The consequence: recruiting recruiters is worth more than recruiting
traders. A branch earns you one person's volume; a tree earns you all of
it.

## Bring an audience

The standard programme is self-serve and open to everyone. If you bring
real distribution - an audience, a community, genuine mindshare - reach
out on [X](https://x.com/BaseStonk): larger arrangements can be
organised, including a vanity code that reads as your name instead of six
random characters.

<Accordion title="Where a referral share comes from">

A referral is paid out of the platform's own cut, never out of the
creator's or holders':

$$
\text{referrer} = \text{platform} \times \rho
$$

so a referred trade pays the creator, holders and the pool exactly what an
unreferred one does. Nothing about the token's economics changes because
somebody arrived through a link.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What a network earns, as a formula">

Your flow is a sum over depths, where each depth's rate applies to the
**whole platform fee** of every trade at that distance:

$$
E = \sum_{d=1}^{5} \rho_d \cdot \Phi_d
\qquad
\Phi_d = \sum_{i \,\in\, \mathcal{N}_d} \min\!\left(\tfrac{\tau_i}{2},\ 100\ \text{bps}\right) \cdot V_i
$$

where $\mathcal{N}_d$ is your network at depth $d$, and $V_i$, $\tau_i$
are each trade's volume and tax. The rates $\rho_1 > \rho_2 > \dots >
\rho_5$ fall with distance - but note what the formula is *not*:

$$
E \ne \sum_{d} \rho^{\,d} \cdot \Phi_d
$$

There is no compounding of fractions down the chain. A share of the same
fee at every level is what keeps depth five worth having.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why a tree outpays a branch">

If each person you bring goes on to bring $k$ more, your network at depth
$d$ has $k^{d-1}$ times the people of your first level, and the flow sums
geometrically:

$$
E \;\propto\; \sum_{d=1}^{5} \rho_d \, k^{\,d-1} \bar{V}
$$

where $\bar{V}$ is average volume per member. For any $k > 1$ the deep
terms dominate despite their smaller rates - five people who each build
are worth more than fifty who only trade.

The same summation is a variance argument: one trader's volume is one
random variable, a five-level network's is a sum over many. Individual
quiet weeks wash out, and what remains is the network's aggregate
activity - the same reason the [platform basket](/bstonk) is steadier
than any single token's stream.

</Accordion>
