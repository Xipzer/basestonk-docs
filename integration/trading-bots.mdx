This page is the assembly order, and it is the same on Base and on
Robinhood Chain: pick the chain once, read that chain's token record,
sign against that chain's router. The pieces it assembles each live on a
reference page - endpoints on the [REST API](/integration/api), contract
mechanics on the [on-chain page](/integration/onchain), addresses for
both chains on the [contract reference](/integration/contracts).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the token's record from the API">
    `GET /api/launchpad/tokens/{address}?chain={chain}` gives the pair
    token, the hook (`curve`), and the tax shape (`buyTaxBps` /
    `sellTaxBps`, or `taxBps` on pre-V6 launches). Respect the
    [rate limits](/integration/api#rate-limits) - one batched call beats
    a per-item loop, and a 429 means refused, never empty.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Construct the PoolKey">
    `(token, pair, 3000, 60, hook)` - the
    [full shape and the hook-per-generation trap](/integration/onchain#constructing-the-poolkey).
    The hook is part of the pool's identity; assume one hook for every
    pool and the other generations chart flat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Quote with the live rate, not the stored one">
    The tax is taken inside the swap, and a fresh V6 launch may be inside
    its anti-sniper window - up to 99%, decaying. `taxFor(key,
    zeroForOne)` on the V6 hook returns what this direction pays right
    now. Set `minOut` with at least that much headroom.
    [The three things every integration must handle](/integration/onchain#three-things-every-integration-must-handle).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Swap through the router">
    Exact-in, approve first:
    [the router](/integration/onchain#the-router). Wallets cannot call
    the v4 PoolManager directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Handle the reverts by name">
    `CurrencyNotSettled` is *reduce size*, `HopTooThin` is the pair's
    pricing route refusing - and it is raised by a contract the launcher
    calls, so match selector `0xb5377e60`.
    [The revert table](/integration/onchain#decoding-reverts).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Launching from a bot rather than trading? The tuple, the salt binding and
the launcher's constraints are on the
[on-chain page](/integration/onchain#launching-programmatically).

Or skip the assembly: `POST /api/v1/agent/trade/prepare` and
`/api/v1/agent/launch/prepare` run this exact recipe server-side - the
record, the key, the live-rate quote, the simulation - and hand back the
unsigned transaction with a verdict of what was checked. Your key still
does the signing. [The AI agents page](/integration/agents) is the
contract.

If a safety scanner flags a token you are evaluating, most of the common
flags are heuristics meeting a v4 architecture they were not written
for - [what each flag actually means here](/token-safety).
