### Linked libraries

The launcher and hook delegate to six libraries deployed alongside them.
They hold no state and take no constructor arguments, but a builder
reproducing a launch's bytecode needs their addresses.

The launcher, hook and factories are bound to each other immutably in both
directions - the hook only configures pools for this launcher, the rewards
and vesting factories only answer this launcher's calls - so any one of
these addresses proves the rest. The RoutedQuoteGate, SwapRouter and
PairOracleRegistry carry over from V5 unchanged.

What V6 added: separate buy and sell tax rates, the
[dev-buy vesting vault](/vesting), the anti-sniper toll, a payout basket
controller seat with renounce-at-launch, and
[community takeover through LaunchControl](/cto).

## Pool parameters

Every BaseStonk pool uses fee `3000` and tick spacing `60`, with the
launcher's hook installed. The full PoolKey shape, the launch tuple and
the events each contract emits are on the
[on-chain page](/integration/onchain).
