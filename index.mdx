One transaction creates a token, opens its Uniswap v4 pool and starts
trading. No bonding curve. No graduation. No migration.

## Two chains, one venue

| | Base | Robinhood Chain |
|---|---|---|
| Chain id | 8453 | 4663 |
| Pair against | USDC, WETH, cbBTC, tokenized stocks, ecosystem tokens, \$BSTONK - or **any token with a real market** | USDG, WETH, 194 Robinhood stocks |
| Platform fee goes to | \$BSTONK buyback and burn | held as USDG |

[Everything that differs &rarr;](/chains)

## What holders get

Dividends are a **percentage of trading volume**, paid automatically in
up to ten assets the creator chose - stocks, ETFs, majors.

<Tip>
A token routing 3% to holders on \$200k daily volume pays holders about
\$6,000 a day, in the assets themselves.
</Tip>

[Dividend baskets &rarr;](/dividend-baskets)

## What the platform cannot do

| Power | Exists? |
|---|:-:|
| Pause, change fees on, or withdraw liquidity from a live pool | No |
| Mint tokens | No |
| Hold user funds | No |
