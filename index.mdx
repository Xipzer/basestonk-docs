BaseStonk launches tokens on Base. No bonding curve, no graduation:
creating a token opens a real Uniswap v4 pool in the same transaction,
and trading starts immediately.

What you see on a BaseStonk chart is a pool price from the first candle -
not a curve that later migrates somewhere else.

## Dividends in the assets you actually want

A launch here can pay its holders in up to ten assets of the creator's
choosing - tokenized stocks, ETFs, majors, ecosystem tokens - delivered
automatically as people trade.

The dividends are a percentage of **trading volume**, not a slice of an
LP fee. A token routing 3% to holders on \$200k daily volume delivers
roughly **\$2.2M of real stock a year**.
[How dividend baskets work &rarr;](/dividend-baskets)

## The dual flywheel

\$BSTONK, the platform's own token, has **two buyback-and-burn engines**.

The first is ordinary: a share of its own trading buys and burns it,
inside the swap.

The second is not. **The platform's cut of every launch on the venue** is
converted to \$BSTONK and burnt, automatically. A token that has nothing
to do with \$BSTONK still burns \$BSTONK simply by trading.

<Note>
**Over 9.6% of supply is already gone** - more than 96 million tokens
burnt, and the number only moves one way.
</Note>

Holders are paid twice over too: **USDC dividends** from \$BSTONK's own
trading, plus a share of the **platform basket vault** - a portfolio of
62 asset slots fed by launches across the whole venue.

## Pick what your token is priced in

Launches choose their quote asset:

- **Stables and majors** - USDC, USDT, WETH, cbBTC
- **Base-native tokens** - VIRTUAL, AERO
- **Tokenized stocks** - shares of NVIDIA, Coinbase, MicroStrategy,
  SpaceX and more
- **Ecosystem tokens** - any admitted Clanker, Virtuals or Bankr token,
  several hundred pairs
- **\$BSTONK** - the platform's own token

A stock-paired token's chart moves with the stock. Whatever the pair, the
opening price is read on-chain at the moment you sign - never typed in,
never taken on trust. [How stock pairs work &rarr;](/stock-pairs)

## What we cannot do

The claims above are only worth what the contracts enforce, so here is
the list of powers that do not exist:

- **We cannot touch a live pool.** No pause, no fee change, no liquidity
  withdrawal.
- **We cannot mint.** Launched tokens have no mint function and no owner.
- **We cannot hold your funds.** Trading is wallet-to-pool through a
  router that custodies nothing.

Every contract is source-verified on Basescan - see the
[contract reference](/integration/contracts).
